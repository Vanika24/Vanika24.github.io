I double majored in Mathematics and Economics at [Lafayette College](https://www.lafayette.edu/), graduating with Honors in Economics in May 2026. My research interests lie in applied microeconomics, econometrics, development economics, and labor economics. During my undergraduate journey at Lafayette College, I worked on policy-relevant research using advanced econometric tools and large-scale datasets such as the National Longitudinal Surveys (NLSY97), DHS, and World Bank household surveys. As a research assistant, I collaborated with faculty on projects ranging from crop diversification and household welfare in Nigeria to econometric modeling with Python, R, and Stata. In Summer 2025, I was selected as a Summer Program for Undergraduate Research (SPUR) intern. SPUR is a nationally competitive opportunity that provides undergraduate students with an intensive 10-week summer research experience under the mentorship of University of Utah faculty members. 

In Summer 2026, I joined [UNOPS](https://www.unops.org/) as a Digital & Innovation Strategy (AI, GIS, & IM) Intern, working at the intersection of technology and international development in their New York office.

Outside of research, I serve as a teaching assistant for Econometrics and Microeconomics. I also mentor high school students in Madagascar through the Lafayette Initiative for Malagasy Education (LIME), 11 freshmen in the Class of 2029 at Lafayette College, served as Director of Finance for the Cambodian Student Association in the United States, and organized Cambodia's first Higher Education Fair. These leadership experiences shaped my passion for mentorship, collaboration, and building opportunities for others.

Besides academics and research, I enjoy playing piano, backpacking, hiking, and running in nature.

I hope, in the future, I can bring together data-driven analysis, policy relevance, and real-world application to tackle complex economic questions and help businesses and organizations make better-informed decisions.
